Scope of results

The tool records browser focus semantics and ARIA live-region changes. Those signals are not equivalent to speech produced by NVDA, VoiceOver, JAWS, or another assistive technology. A matching transcript is not proof of WCAG conformance and does not replace testing with disabled people or real assistive technology.
